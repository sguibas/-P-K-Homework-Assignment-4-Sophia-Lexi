# Calibration Equations

## Normalized reading

The baseline is $b_0$.

$$
r = \frac{x-b_0}{s} \tag{1}
$$

## Combined uncertainty

Use the two component terms.

$$
u = \sqrt{u_1^2 + u_2^2} \tag{2}
$$
